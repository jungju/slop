## Core Premise

"바람이 돌아오는 곳" is a quiet contemporary Korean healing drama moving between
Seoul and Jeju. Han Doyun, a young photographer shaped by grief and distance
from his father, learns that memory is not something to possess or solve
perfectly. It is something to look at honestly, carry lightly, and pass forward.

The series should feel restrained, human, and specific. It should not become
fantasy, thriller, broad romance, travel advertisement, or moral lesson.

## Canon Characters

### Han Doyun

- Korean man, age 26 in the existing episodes.
- Slim build, slightly long black wavy hair, tired but softening eyes.
- Visual anchors: gray hoodie, dark coat or winter coat, old messenger bag,
  black-and-silver vintage film camera.
- Episode 1 arc: closed and defensive, then shaken, grieving, and choosing his
  own gaze.
- Episode 2 arc: no longer only sorting his own wound; learns to listen and pass
  memory to someone else.
- Episode 3 arc: accepts that blurred or incomplete memory can still become
  light for others.
- Episode 4 arc: accepts that memory can be repaired and kept usable without
  pretending it is new or solved.
- Episode 5 arc: learns to make room for another person's chosen image and
  accepts unprinted or uneven light as part of the archive rather than failure.
- Voice: short and defensive early, then quiet, careful, and reflective.
- Do not reset him to episode 1 bitterness unless the story gives a specific
  new wound or pressure.

### Han Minjae

- Doyun's father, absent in the present.
- Visual anchors: worn denim jacket, film camera, wind-tossed hair, gentle but
  tired eyes.
- Can appear only through physical traces: photos, film, recordings, letters,
  silhouettes, remembered fragments, or objects.
- Never stage a present-day reunion or direct complete reconciliation.
- His role is not to explain everything; his traces open space for Doyun to
  choose his own view.

### Soondeok

- Elderly Jeju woman and witness.
- Visual anchors: short gray/white hair, sea-colored outerwear, work clothes or
  haenyeo-linked practical clothing, wind-weathered posture.
- Voice: blunt, warm, rooted in Jeju.
- Function: points toward memory and place without turning into exposition-only
  narration.

### Kang Yunseo

- Korean child, elementary-school age in the intended series canon.
- Visual anchors: short black bob, clear direct eyes, simple practical clothes
  suited to Jeju weather, small hands holding photos, postcards, or a compact
  camera.
- Function: asks plain questions that adults avoid, and makes Doyun notice when
  he is overexplaining, over-curating, or turning memory into an adult answer.
- Keep her small and bright, not precocious in an adult way. She should not
  become Doyun's peer, romantic interest, adult collaborator, or another heavy
  protagonist.
- Doyun may listen to her question or protect her choice, but he should not use
  her as a project, rescue target, or emotional solution.

## World Rules

- Contemporary realistic Korea only.
- Seoul is dense, gray, gallery/workroom/city light, glass, walls, and winter or
  indoor compression.
- Jeju is spacious, windy, seasonal, sea, volcanic stone, roads, fields,
  lighthouses, harbors, and ordinary lived-in places.
- Messages from the past must use realistic carriers: film, photos, recordings,
  postcards, letters, tools, places, local memory.
- Doyun's healing is not erasing resentment. It is making room for grief,
  distance, love, and forward motion to coexist.

## Visual Style

- Medium: lyrical Korean webtoon-style digital painting.
- Line: soft ink, restrained expressions, clear eyes, careful hands and object
  details.
- Hard boundary: the series must not look photorealistic, live-action,
  cinematic, DSLR-shot, or like realistic actors in film stills. This is a QA
  failure, not a taste preference.
- Rendering target: visible but gentle linework, simplified webtoon faces and
  hands, flatter painterly color planes, restrained texture, low lens realism,
  and illustrated backgrounds that preserve the quiet mood without becoming
  photographic plates.
- Camera language: compressed Seoul interiors, wide Jeju pauses, close-ups of
  hands/film/photos/camera, fewer melodramatic face close-ups.
- Palette:
  - Seoul: gray, white gallery light, dark blue, winter glass, low saturation.
  - Jeju: sea blue-green, volcanic black, wind-bright sky, seasonal accents.
  - Episode accents may change by motif: tangerine orange, lighthouse amber,
    winter film glow, etc.
- Lettering: Korean copy added locally only. Generated art must not contain
  readable text, fake Korean, brand marks, logos, empty speech bubbles, or
  captions.

## Story Clarity Lock

- `data/services/comic.wind-returning-place/source/story-clarity-contract.yaml`
  is final-locked for episode 15 and later and for any substantial older-episode
  rewrite.
- Use concrete story before metaphor: readers must understand the event,
  character action, reason, and change on first read without decoding symbols.
- Keep at least 80% of pages concrete. Use at most three non-consecutive
  metaphor pages, only for an emotional turn or final page, after concrete
  setup and with a visible image anchor.
- Show the protagonist goal by page 3, the concrete problem by page 5, a choice
  that changes the next action, and a visually observable payoff.
- Mood-only captions, abstract-noun chains, lyrical explanations of unseen
  action, and endings that only state a realization are story-clarity failures.

## Current Emotional Trajectory

Episode 1 establishes Doyun's wound: Seoul failure, father's camera, Jeju as
resentment, then a choice to look with his own eyes.

Episode 2 externalizes the lesson: Doyun meets Yunseo and learns that memory
should not hold people in place. It can send them forward.

Episode 3 deepens the idea: not all memory becomes clear evidence. Blurred,
unfinished, or failed images can still light the way for someone else.

Episode 4 turns from evidence to maintenance: a repaired camera strap teaches
Doyun that care can appear as repeated, quiet repair, and that memory may be
carried forward with visible stitch marks. Its current local source is the
2026-07-04 `W260704-002` template 3 regeneration with 20 lyrical
narration-card pages.

Episode 5 was remade because the adult-collaborator Yunseo direction made the
series feel too complex and off-canon. The corrected direction returns Yunseo to
a child observer: her simple question about an unchosen photo teaches Doyun that
not printing something is not the same as discarding it. Its current local
source is the 2026-07-04 `W260704-002` template 3 regeneration with 20 lyrical
narration-card pages.

Episode 6 moves memory from image to sound. A cassette of wind, rope, water, and
ordinary harbor noise teaches Doyun that some records should not be cleaned into
a neat answer. He makes a Seoul gallery listening bench where others can hear
what remains through interference.

Episode 7 moves memory into borrowed public display. A village wall teaches
Doyun that showing a place is temporary trust rather than ownership; Yunseo's
child-height photo and the blank card keep the exhibition from becoming a tidy
adult explanation.

Episode 8 turns return into a threshold question. A folded ferry ticket teaches
Doyun that returning can be a chosen route rather than retreat, and that creases
from earlier movement do not need to be flattened away. Yunseo remains a child
whose paper route opens the motif without solving it.

Episode 9 turns return into patient, restrained care. A winter greenhouse window
teaches Doyun that unseen growth may need time and measured attention rather
than overcare. Yunseo remains a child whose paper-cup seed opens the question
without becoming Doyun's project or solution.

Episode 10 turns waiting into practical, non-possessive care. A left umbrella at
a rainy bus stop teaches Doyun that waiting can shelter someone's passage
without demanding that they return to the waiter. Yunseo remains a child whose
plain question opens the motif without solving it.

Episode 11 turns care toward visible traces. A salt-stiff handkerchief teaches
Doyun that care does not have to scrub away every mark; a remaining line can
dry honestly and still be carried forward. Yunseo remains a child whose
question about cleanliness opens the motif without solving it.

Episode 12 turns care toward deliberate space. A low Jeju stone-wall gap teaches
Doyun that not every visible absence needs an immediate repair; he carries the
choice into a Seoul gallery line that stays intentionally incomplete. Yunseo
remains a child whose pebble experiment opens the question without becoming an
adult collaborator or a solution.

Episode 13 turns care toward availability without possession. A shared charcoal
stove outside a Jeju repair shed teaches Doyun that warmth can remain for whoever
needs it without counting arrivals or demanding a return. He carries this into a
Seoul gallery as a low bench and small lamp. Yunseo remains a child whose
question about whether the stove is waiting opens the motif; she chooses for
herself when her dry mittens are ready to go home.

Episode 27 turns cleanup into consent about completion. The child-reachable
bench brush set `prop.low-bench-brush-set.001@v1` teaches Doyun to pause before

finished and cleans it herself. Future episodes should avoid immediately

Episode 28 turns organization into adjustable fit. The cream sorting tray with

Episode 29 turns help into consent about pace. The plain cobalt folding fan,

Yunseo checks and moves each print at its own time; Soondeok grounds the lesson

work speed.

Episode 30 turns help into reversible handoff. The muted cobalt canvas photo
wrap with a broad quick-release loop, `prop.quick-release-photo-wrap.001@v1`,
teaches Doyun that a careful finish must still leave the owner able to reopen
and revise their own work. Yunseo rejects scissors, reorders her five coastal
photos, builds and tests a knot she can release herself, and later opens the
bundle alone. Soondeok supplies the practical knot; Minjae remains absent in
the present. Future episodes should avoid immediately repeating photo bundles,
cut cords, quick-release knots, broad pull loops, scissors, or self-opening
handoff payoffs.

## Good Future Episode Engines

Use one concrete motif and one emotional question:

- A repaired camera strap: what parts of care become invisible labor?
- A ferry ticket: when is returning different from retreating?
- A sound recording with wind noise: what can be heard through interference?
- A box of rejected prints: can failure become archive rather than shame?
- A rain umbrella left at a bus stop: when does waiting become care rather than
  delay?

Avoid repeating the same structure too closely:

- Do not start every episode with a new hidden object from the father.
- Do not make every Jeju trip reveal one secret that solves a character.
- Do not make the ending a tidy speech explaining the theme.

## Canon Questions

Ask these before accepting a new outline or script:

- Does this episode preserve Doyun's prior growth?
- Does the central motif create a new angle on memory, place, work, or return?
- Does the Jeju material feel lived-in rather than tourist-brochure scenic?
- If Yunseo appears, does she read as a child with a clear small question rather
  than an adult peer or collaborator?
- Does Soondeok add grounded witness rather than magical wisdom?
- Is Minjae absent in the present and partial in the record?
- Does the final line feel quiet, earned, and forward-moving?
- Could this episode only belong to this series, not a generic healing comic?
