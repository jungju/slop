# 바람이 돌아오는 곳 13: 온기가 남는 자리

## Motif and continuity

At a small Jeju boat-repair shed, a shared charcoal stove is kept outside the door for any cold hands that pass it. It offers warmth without asking who will return. Doyun carries that ethic into a Seoul gallery as a simple warm bench beside an unfinished viewing wall.

This advances episode 12's chosen space without repeating its stone wall, wind gap, or gallery-line structure. Yunseo remains a child with one plain question; Soondeok has the grounded, practical position that the stove is not a promise. Han Minjae remains absent in the present.

## Twenty beats

1. A rain-dark Seoul gallery closes early; Doyun's cold hands hover over an unplugged kettle.
2. A photo from Soondeok shows an old stove outside a Jeju repair shed.
3. Her short message says the stove has been lit because the north wind has started.
4. Doyun arrives at the low shed, where a dented charcoal stove glows by the doorway.
5. A fisherman silently warms his palms, then leaves before Doyun can ask his name.
6. Doyun assumes the stove belongs to the shed owner.
7. Soondeok corrects him: it belongs to the cold, not to a person.
8. The stove top holds a plain kettle; steam folds into the wind.
9. Yunseo comes with rain-damp mittens and sets them near, not on, the stove.
10. She asks whether the stove is waiting for someone.
11. Soondeok says a warm place can be there without counting who comes.
12. Doyun notices a row of different hands—old, young, work-worn—briefly near the heat.
13. He photographs only their sleeves and palms, leaving faces private.
14. Yunseo moves her mittens back when they are dry and says they can go home now.
15. Doyun returns to Seoul with the quiet image of steam against the open door.
16. In the gallery, he places a low wooden bench beside the viewing wall and a plain warm lamp above it.
17. No sign tells visitors to sit; one visitor rests their hands there anyway.
18. Doyun pins the anonymous hand photographs beside the bench, with open space between them.
19. After closing, he turns off the gallery lights but leaves the small bench lamp on for the cleaner.
20. The warm place remains: not a call to return, but a place that does not hurry anyone away.
