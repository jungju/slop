# 바람이 돌아오는 곳 14: 열린 서랍

## Motif and continuity

A worn key tag leads Doyun to an unlocked shared drawer in a Jeju harbor house. The drawer holds ordinary things that are useful only because no person claims them for long. He carries the practice to Seoul as an open gallery drawer.

This advances episode 13's available warmth into practical access without repeating the stove, bench, anonymous-hand series, waiting, or repair structure. Yunseo remains a child with one concrete question and her own choice. Soondeok keeps a practical stance. Minjae is absent in the present.

## Twenty beats

1. Doyun finds a worn key tag in a Seoul archive tray.
2. Its blank metal holds only a ring worn by many hands.
3. Soondeok identifies it as belonging to a Jeju shared drawer.
4. Doyun returns to a windy harbor lane.
5. The shared drawer is open and unmarked.
6. He sees gloves, rope, and a flashlight inside.
7. Soondeok returns the tag to the drawer rather than giving it away.
8. Doyun tries to close the drawer from worry.
9. Yunseo asks who the flashlight belongs to.
10. A rain-soaked rider borrows it and leaves.
11. The empty drawer remains useful, not abandoned.
12. Doyun photographs objects and their open places instead of faces.
13. Soondeok keeps the tag with the shared supplies.
14. Yunseo hangs her wet hood, then takes it back herself when it dries.
15. On the ferry, Doyun opens his hand around the tag.
16. He makes a shallow gallery drawer in Seoul.
17. He adds a pencil, flashlight, blank sleeve, and small map.
18. A visitor borrows the flashlight; Doyun records the hand from a distance.
19. At closing, Doyun leaves the drawer slightly open.
20. A new hand reaches into the drawer while Doyun lets the space remain unclaimed.
