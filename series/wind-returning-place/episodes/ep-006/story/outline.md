# 바람이 돌아오는 곳 6: 바람의 녹음

## Intent

Episode 6 moves from visual memory into recorded sound. Doyun does not uncover a
new father secret or solve someone else's life. He learns to leave wind noise in
place so other people can hear what survives through interference.

## Motif

- Core object: an old cassette tape and portable recorder.
- Emotional question: can noise carry memory without being cleaned into a neat
  answer?
- Setting rhythm: Seoul gallery archive -> Jeju community room and harbor ->
  Seoul listening bench.

## 20-Beat Outline

1. Doyun finds a small box of old cassette tapes in the gallery archive.
2. He handles one unmarked tape as carefully as a photograph.
3. The first playback is mostly wind, rope, water, and broken fragments.
4. Instead of forcing an answer, he calls Soondeok for context.
5. He returns to Jeju with the recorder wrapped like a fragile object.
6. Soondeok recognizes the tapes as village recordings, not a private secret.
7. Yunseo asks whether wind can also be a voice.
8. The three walk toward the harbor where the sounds were probably made.
9. Doyun listens to rope, water, and work as part of the recording.
10. Soondeok refuses to translate the noise into a tidy explanation.
11. Yunseo listens to a shell/stone and makes the question small again.
12. Doyun accepts that interference can be part of the record.
13. Back in Seoul, he prepares a listening bench beside the photos.
14. He chooses space and headphones instead of explanatory wall text.
15. A visitor listens without needing Doyun's interpretation.
16. Yunseo contributes a small Jeju stone to the listening space.
17. Doyun decides not to remove all the noise from the tape.
18. Visitors leave the headphones carefully, carrying their own hearing.
19. Doyun sits with the recording after the gallery empties.
20. He carries the recorder into dawn, understanding that noisy wind can return
    as a voice.

## Story Gates

- Series repetition: PASS. The episode uses sound recording rather than hidden
  photos, repair work, or rejected contact sheets.
- Reader engagement: PASS. Curiosity begins with the cassette, turns at the
  harbor when the tape becomes ordinary labor instead of a secret, and pays off
  through the listening bench.
- Secondary-character agency: PASS. Soondeok refuses neat explanation; Yunseo
  asks a child-scale question and contributes the stone.
- Canon check: PASS. Minjae does not appear in the present; Doyun listens and
  frames instead of solving; Yunseo remains a child.
