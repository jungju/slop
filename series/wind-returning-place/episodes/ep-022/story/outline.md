# 바람이 돌아오는 곳 22: 잡을 수 있는 선

1. Doyun coils a blue-and-cream guide rope in his Seoul workroom.
2. A photograph shows slippery Jeju harbor steps after rain.
3. He brings the removable rope to Jeju.
4. Yunseo stops before the wet steps.
5. Doyun clips the rope high between two posts.
6. Yunseo cannot comfortably reach it.
7. Soondeok says a line no one can hold is only decoration.
8. Doyun removes the rope instead of defending the first placement.
9. Yunseo marks the height of her own open hand.
10. Soondeok pulls the rope with both hands to test the hooks.
11. The loose middle sags toward the wet ground.
12. Doyun shortens the span and adds a middle loop.
13. Yunseo checks the tension with one hand.
14. She chooses to grip the red stitched loop.
15. Yunseo crosses the steps under her own power.
16. Soondeok unclips the rope when the path dries.
17. Back in Seoul, Doyun installs low removable anchor points by a ramp.
18. He clips the same kind of guide rope at reachable height.
19. A visitor takes the line without asking for directions.
20. Doyun leaves the line within reach, not across the way.
