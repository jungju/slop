# 바람이 돌아오는 곳 10: 남겨진 우산

- Episode: `ep-010`
- Template: Comic Factory template 3, lyrical narration-card comic
- Format: 20 pages / 20 panels
- Core motif: a rain umbrella left at a bus stop and later translated into a gallery waiting bench
- Emotional question: when does waiting become care rather than delay?
- Setting rhythm: Seoul rainy gallery entrance -> Jeju rainy bus stop -> Seoul gallery threshold

## Continuity State

- Doyun is no longer solving people through objects. He listens, frames, and makes a practical space for memory to pass through.
- Yunseo remains a child. Her question stays small and direct, and she does not become an adult collaborator or romantic peer.
- Soondeok remains a grounded Jeju witness with practical friction, not a comforting explainer.
- Minjae remains absent in the present and is not used as a new clue.
- The episode avoids the immediately recent motifs: cassette/sound cleanup, borrowed wall, folded ticket/ferry threshold, winter greenhouse, paper-cup seed, water-marked gallery window, and overwatering restraint.

## Twenty-Beat Outline

1. A left umbrella waits at the Seoul gallery entrance longer than the rain.
2. Doyun notices water on the handle before trying to identify an owner.
3. Soondeok, by phone, refuses to make the umbrella into a tidy clue.
4. Doyun carries the umbrella to a rainy Jeju bus stop.
5. Soondeok says waiting does not always mean holding someone in place.
6. Child Yunseo asks whether waiting means someone must come back.
7. Doyun looks at the empty road instead of answering quickly.
8. A late traveler asks to borrow the umbrella rather than reclaim it.
9. The bus leaves, and the shelter keeps the shape of non-possessive waiting.
10. Doyun photographs the empty road and wet seat.
11. Yunseo folds her own small umbrella and asks whether care can be left behind.
12. Yunseo draws a small umbrella in fogged glass; the mark fades.
13. Back in Seoul, Doyun places a low bench and umbrella stand near the gallery entrance.
14. The display gives visitors a place to pause without explanation.
15. Visitors set down umbrellas before looking at the photographs.
16. Yunseo's small umbrella photo rests at child height.
17. A visitor remembers waiting that was care, not delay.
18. Umbrella shadows make the gallery floor resemble the Jeju shelter.
19. Doyun leaves one spare umbrella without writing an owner's name.
20. The final image holds the umbrella stand, open door, camera, and forward rain-light.

## Story Gates

- Series-engine repetition gate: PASS. The motif changes from greenhouse/restraint to umbrella/waiting care, with a different carrier, setting rhythm, and emotional question.
- Reader engagement lap: PASS. Micro-tension comes from the unknown owner and the borrowed umbrella; the turn is the late traveler borrowing rather than reclaiming; the payoff is a gallery threshold that lets care be used without ownership.
- Secondary-character agency/friction: PASS. Soondeok refuses a tidy explanation; Yunseo asks a child-scale question and contributes a small photo; neither solves Doyun's feeling for him.
- Father boundary: PASS. Minjae is absent and not used as an explanatory clue.
