# 바람이 돌아오는 곳 9: 겨울 온실

## Intent

Episode 9 moves the series from return as route/threshold into return as slow
growth that can happen while the season looks empty. Doyun does not solve a
hidden clue or explain another person. He learns to leave room, wait without
overwatering, and let a quiet record remain partial.

## Motif

- Core object/place: winter greenhouse window, water marks, small sprouts,
  watering can, paper cup seed.
- Emotional question: what grows while people think nothing is happening?
- Avoided structures: cassette/recording cleanup, borrowed public wall, folded
  ticket/ferry threshold, repaired strap, rejected contact sheet, new father
  secret.

## Structure

1. Setup: a neglected Seoul gallery plant and window water marks.
2. Search: Doyun visits a Jeju winter greenhouse after Soondeok's grounded
   comment.
3. Turn: Yunseo's child question reframes hidden growth without making her an
   adult collaborator.
4. Return: Doyun brings the greenhouse idea back as a quiet gallery window,
   not a tidy explanation.
5. Payoff: one careful watering and the closing image of unseen growth.

## Story Gates

- Series-engine repetition: PASS. Greenhouse/window/growth differs from the
  last three motifs: sound tape, borrowed wall, folded ferry ticket.
- Reader engagement: PASS. The episode carries micro-tension through the dying
  plant, invisible growth, risk of overcare, and the delayed payoff of one
  measured watering.
- Secondary-character agency/friction: PASS. Soondeok resists overcare with
  practical greenhouse knowledge. Yunseo asks a small child-scale question and
  plants a seed in a paper cup without becoming Doyun's emotional solution.
- Father boundary: PASS. Minjae does not appear in the present and is not used
  as a new clue.
