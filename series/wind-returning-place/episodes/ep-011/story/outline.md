# 바람이 돌아오는 곳 11: 소금빛 손수건

## Emotional State

- Doyun continues as a listener and framer after episode 10, not a solver.
- Yunseo remains a child whose plain question opens the motif.
- Soondeok keeps practical friction and refuses spotless explanations.
- Minjae remains absent from present-day action and is not used as a new clue.

## Motif

A salt-stiff handkerchief turns care from sheltering passage into allowing traces to remain visible. The episode asks whether clean care means erasing evidence, or letting a mark dry honestly.

3. The salt mark remains when he lifts the cloth into gallery light.

20. Final still life of handkerchief, camera, cloth screen, and morning doorway light.
