# Episode 24 Outline — 물이 머무는 받침

- Setup 1-4: Doyun finds a shallow ceramic plant saucer and brings it from Seoul to the Jeju greenhouse worktable.
- Friction 5-8: He mistakes retained water for neglect; Yunseo stops him and chooses a visible safe water line.
- Practical turn 9-13: Soondeok distinguishes useful waiting from stagnation; Doyun opens the drain notch while Yunseo rotates and checks the pot herself.
- Choice 14-16: Yunseo decides when absorption is sufficient and empties the remaining water herself.
- Return/payoff 17-20: In Seoul, Doyun leaves plant-specific thresholds rather than one rule; a visitor checks soil and decides independently.

The episode avoids the prior timer/rope/hook mechanisms. Its new dramatic question is whether Doyun can observe a useful pause without erasing it in the name of tidiness.
