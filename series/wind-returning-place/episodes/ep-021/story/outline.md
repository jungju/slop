# Episode 21 outline — 펴 둔 걸이

- Setup 1–3: Doyun folds a raincoat hook flat in Seoul; a wet forecast and matching Jeju wall photo establish the practical question.
- Problem 4–7: Yunseo arrives wet at the Jeju repair shed; the folded hook leaves her coat on a chair and blocks the next task.
- Action 8–12: Yunseo opens the hook herself; Soondeok adds the wet-load constraint; Doyun measures and finds a matching hinge plate.
- Turn 13–16: Doyun installs and load-tests the open hook; Yunseo chooses the height and uses it without help.
- Return 17–19: Doyun installs the same hook in Seoul and a visitor uses it before entering the workroom.
- Payoff 20: the open hook carries a drying coat while Doyun leaves the passage clear.

Series-engine check: unlike ep-018–020, this is not about adjusting a window, leaving capacity, or lowering access hardware. The concrete conflict is a folded shared drying fixture that turns wet coats into clutter; the solution is to leave a safe, load-bearing hook available for real use.
