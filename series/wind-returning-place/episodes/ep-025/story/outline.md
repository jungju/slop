# 바람이 돌아오는 곳 25: 돌려 맞춘 빛

## Setup — pages 1-4

Doyun sees glare erase detail from a glossy print in Seoul, then takes an adjustable blue clamp lamp to a Jeju repair shed where Yunseo is sorting damp image cards at a low workbench.

## Friction — pages 5-8

He clamps the lamp high and aims it straight down. The wet surfaces flare white, Yunseo cannot distinguish the images, and the loose wing nut lets the shade slip when she tries to turn it.

## Practical turn — pages 9-12

Soondeok compares glare on a wet print and a matte backing card. Doyun repairs the pivot only enough to hold its position while keeping it movable; Yunseo shifts the clamp to her side of the table.

## Choice — pages 13-16

Yunseo chooses a low sideways angle, sorts one image successfully, then turns the same lamp again for a darker card. The prop proves useful because its angle can change with the work.

## Return and payoff — pages 17-20

Doyun installs two reachable adjustable lamps along a Seoul print table. Two visitors choose different angles without asking him. He continues his own work and lets the light follow their hands rather than fixing their gaze.

## Repetition gate

- New motif: adjustable clamp task lamp, not the prior saucer, timer, guide rope, hook, handle, or drying line.
- New carrier: practical glare and work-surface visibility, not a found father clue or hidden record.
- New rhythm: Seoul glare test -> Jeju shared repair/sorting bench -> Seoul parallel user-controlled workstations.
- New emotional question: can Doyun illuminate a task without deciding another person's viewpoint?

## Engagement and agency

- Micro-tension: the image disappears under apparently helpful light.
- Curiosity gap: the lamp is not broken as an object; its usefulness depends on angle and user.
- Quiet surprise: Yunseo needs two different angles for two different surfaces.
- Visible payoff: two Seoul visitors independently set different lamp angles.
- Yunseo changes the procedure twice; Soondeok supplies a material test; Doyun repairs but does not direct the final use.
