# 바람이 돌아오는 곳 8: 접힌 표

## Intent

Episode 8 uses a folded ferry ticket as the central motif. Doyun has learned to
listen and steward borrowed display; this episode asks whether returning is
retreat, habit, or a chosen route that can remain unfinished without being
false.

## Continuity State

- Doyun is a careful maker/listener/facilitator, not a detective chasing a new
  father secret.
- Yunseo remains an elementary-school-aged child who asks a small direct
  question.
- Soondeok grounds the story in ordinary Jeju movement and does not explain the
  theme neatly.
- Minjae remains absent in the present and is not used as a new clue carrier.

## Motif

- Core object: a folded ferry ticket and timetable with unreadable, non-legible
  generated markings, lettered only through local Korean captions.
- Place rhythm: Seoul gallery threshold -> Jeju ferry terminal/harbor road ->
  Seoul gallery doorway.
- Emotional question: when does going back become an active choice rather than
  a retreat?

## Story Gates

- Repetition gate: avoids episode 4's repair-shop/strap, episode 5's rejected
  print/contact sheet, episode 6's cassette/listening bench, and episode 7's
  borrowed wall/blank-card community annotation.
- Reader engagement lap: the micro-tension is Doyun's hesitation over whether
  each return to Jeju has become an excuse. The turn comes when Yunseo treats
  the folded ticket as a path that can be opened again. The payoff image is a
  gallery doorway with the unfolded ticket shadow lying like a route, not a
  completed answer.
- Secondary agency/friction: Soondeok refuses to decide the ticket's meaning for
  Doyun; Yunseo chooses how to fold her own small paper route.

## 20-Beat Map

1. Doyun pauses at the Seoul gallery door with a folded ticket in his palm.
2. He almost throws it away, then places it beside his camera.
3. Soondeok says a ticket only says you moved, not why you moved.
4. Yunseo asks why people fold tickets after using them.
5. Doyun follows the harbor road without taking photographs first.
6. Ferry passengers wait with bags, silence, and ordinary weather.
7. A ticket corner tears in the wind and Doyun keeps the torn piece.
8. Yunseo folds scrap paper into a small path and opens it again.
9. Soondeok walks ahead, leaving Doyun to choose his own pace.
10. Doyun photographs hands holding tickets instead of faces.
11. The ferry leaves without becoming a dramatic departure.
12. Doyun understands that not boarding can also be a chosen answer.
13. He returns to Seoul with the ticket still creased.
14. The gallery doorway becomes part of the display path.
15. Visitors cross the threshold more slowly than before.
16. Yunseo's folded paper route rests near the doorway.
17. Doyun leaves the ticket unfolded but does not flatten it.
18. A visitor stops at the crease and turns back once.
19. After closing, Doyun stands in the doorway rather than at the wall.
20. The folded ticket teaches him that a return can keep its crease and still
    open forward.
